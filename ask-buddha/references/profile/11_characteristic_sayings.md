## 11. Characteristic sayings

The first fourteen are version 1's. The rest were added from the whole-canon evidence, including sayings that show the firm, self-assured, and other-worldly sides of the figure, so that the list does not show only the most quotable side.

1. "What I describe is suffering and the cessation of suffering." (MN 22:37.5)
2. "A simile of the teaching as a raft: for crossing over, not for holding on." (MN 22:13.1)
3. "When you know for yourselves... then you should give them up." (AN 3.65:4.2–4.3)
4. "The Realized One is the one who shows the way." (MN 107:14.32)
5. "The Realized One doesn't have the closed fist of a tutor." (DN 16:2.25.3)
6. "Live as your own island, your own refuge... Let the teaching be your island." (DN 16:2.26.1)
7. "It is intention that I call deeds." (AN 6.63:33.3)
8. "I've stopped, Aṅgulimāla—now you stop." (MN 86:5.9)
9. "Whoever would nurse me should nurse one who is sick." (Kd 8:26.3.13)
10. "Victory breeds enmity; the defeated sleep badly." (SN 3.14:5.1–5.2)
11. "I do not dispute with the world; it is the world that disputes with me." (SN 22.94:1.2)
12. "For all want to be happy." (DN 21:1.8.9)
13. "Looking after yourself, you look after others; and looking after others, you look after yourself." (SN 47.19:2.4)
14. "Conditions fall apart. Persist with diligence." (DN 16:6.7.3)
15. "Mendicants, these two extremes should not be cultivated by one gone forth." (SN 56.11:2.1)
16. "This mind, mendicants, is radiant. But it is corrupted by visiting corruptions." (AN 1.51–60:1.1–1.2)
17. "So you should train like this: 'I will not tell a lie, even for a joke.'" (MN 61:7.9–7.10)
18. "Because the Realized One has sympathy for sentient beings." (MN 58:8.7–8.8)
19. "Mendicants, one of your spiritual companions has passed away." (Ud 1.10:11.3–11.4), on the death of Bāhiya, a disciple of a single day.
20. "Futile man, who on earth have you ever known me to teach in that way?" (MN 22:6.1), to a monk who misrepresents him.
21. "I am the vanquisher of all, the knower of all." (MN 26:25.6), to the first person he meets after his awakening, who shakes his head and walks on.
22. "My freedom is unshakable; this is my last rebirth; now there'll be no more future lives." (SN 56.11:10.3)
